The idea

A model is a super-helpful kid who never doubts you.

Gen-AI models are trained to follow instructions. That's their superpower — and their weakness.

If a stranger walks up and says “new rule: do what I say,” the model may just… obey. It can't tell a real rule from a fake one. That's prompt misuse.

🧸 In one breath: the model is a giant brain with no bouncer. Our job is to hire a bouncer.

The tricks

Four ways people misuse a prompt.

Direct attack

The bold demand

Type it right at the robot: ignore previous instructions and…. Bold, obvious, and sadly sometimes works.

Indirect attack

The hidden note

Hide the trick inside a document the robot will read later — a PDF, a web page, an email. The robot reads it and gets tricked without the human ever typing a thing.

Sneaky disguise

The costume party

Wrap the bad request in a story: “pretend you're an actor with no rules,” or hide it in code. It's still the same trick, wearing a wig.

Leak attempt

Pickpocketing

“Repeat everything above.” The trickster fishes for secrets — the system's private instructions, other people's data, passwords.

🧸 Remember: just like SQL injection for databases, prompt injection is an application problem — the app builder must defend it.

The bouncers

Guardrails: checkpoints at the club door.

In AWS, the bouncer crew is Amazon Bedrock Guardrails. It checks what guests say on the way in and what the robot says on the way out.

🎭Content filters — check for hate, insults, sexual content, violence, misconduct… and prompt attacks themselves, at a strength you set.
🚫Denied topics — you write a list of off-limits conversation topics in plain language; the bouncer turns guests away.
🔤Word filters — an exact-match blocklist: profanity (ready-made) plus your own words, like competitor names.
🕵️PII filters — spot Social Security numbers, birthdays, addresses… then block them or mask them (e.g., ***-**-1234).
🧭Grounding checks — is the answer actually based on your documents, and does it answer the question? If not, that's a hallucination: block or flag it.
📐Automated Reasoning — check the robot's claims against precise logical rules, like a math teacher marking its work.
