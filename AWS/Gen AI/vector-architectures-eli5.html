Where a chatbot's searchable memory lives on AWS

Picture a huge library where books are sorted by meaning, not by title. Here are the AWS ways to build one.

Step 1 · The big idea

Meaning becomes a location

A model turns text into a list of numbers called a vector (an embedding). Text with similar meaning lands close together on a map.

near = similar meaning far = unrelated
Step 2 · Why we need it

RAG: look it up, then answer

A model only knows what it was trained on. RAG (retrieval-augmented generation) lets it check your documents first.

Step 3 · Four ways on AWS

Pick your helper

Same job every time: store vectors, find the nearest ones. The difference is how much AWS does for you.

🤖

Bedrock Knowledge Bases

The auto-chef

Fully managed RAG. You point it at your data, and it handles chunking, embedding, storage and retrieval.

S3 files→chunk + embed→vector store
Least workManaged RAG
⚡

OpenSearch (k-NN)

The speed librarian

A search engine with a vector index (k-NN). Built for large scale, and it can mix meaning search with keyword search.

vectors+keywords→top k matches
Large scaleHybrid search
🐘

RDS / Aurora + pgvector

The spreadsheet with a superpower

Your normal PostgreSQL database stores vectors next to your rows. Filter with SQL and match by meaning in one query.

WHERE dept = 'HR'+closest vectors
One databaseSQL filters
🧩

DynamoDB vector search

The key desk that can now search by meaning

DynamoDB stores vectors beside your other attributes and runs approximate nearest-neighbor search, with attribute filters. Generally available as of August 2026.

embed + store item→vector index→ANN query
ServerlessKey-value app data

Step 5 · Gotchas

Things that trip people up

⚠️Old notes saying "DynamoDB can't do vectors" are stale. Native vector search is generally available now. Check current docs before you design around it.
✂️Chunking matters more than the store. Bad chunks give bad answers in every option.
🔁Embed queries and documents with the same model. Mixing models breaks the map.
🧭Exact supported stores and limits change. Confirm what the Bedrock KB supports and the index limits for your chosen store in the current AWS docs.
Step 6 · TL;DR

If you remember one thing

your stuff→embeddings→vector store→model answers

Want it fast with no fuss? Bedrock Knowledge Bases. Want SQL? RDS or Aurora with pgvector. Want huge search? OpenSearch. Already on DynamoDB? Try its native vector search.
