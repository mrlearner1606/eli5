Fine print

Gotchas that cost real money ⚠️

  • Cold startServerless and scaled-to-zero endpoints take time to wake up. Kids hate waiting.
  • Over-provisionRenting for peak traffic means paying for empty seats at 3 a.m.
  • Quantize blindlySmaller weights are not always "just as good" — measure quality, don't assume.
  • Batch too hardBigger batches raise throughput but add latency per request.
  • Model too bigIf it doesn't fit, you pay for multi-GPU instances whether you use them or not.
  • No load testOptimizing without measuring is guessing. Load test, then tune.

TL;DR

Five things to remember 🧠

  • 1The model isn't the cost — the time the GPU sits there is the cost.
  • 2Choose the door first: real-time, serverless, async, or batch.
  • 3Then flip levers: batching, quantization, speculative decoding, tensor parallelism, autoscaling.
  • 4Speed and cost fight. Every optimization is a trade you measure, not a checkbox you tick.
